Why Reglazing Beats Replacement

A new bathtub can cost several thousand dollars once demolition, plumbing, tile repair and disposal are added. Reglazing a bathtub often costs a small fraction of that price. Most projects are finished in a few hours, so you can use your bathroom again the next day. There is no dumpster, no broken tile and no weeks of construction dust. If you are searching for affordable bathtub refinishing in New York, reglazing is the fastest way to upgrade a bathroom on a budget.

Reglazing also keeps your existing layout. Cast iron and porcelain tubs are heavy and hard to remove from older apartments and pre war buildings. Refinishing the tub in place protects walls, floors and neighbors from disruption. It is a practical choice for co-ops, condos and rentals where building rules limit renovation work.

Countertop Refinishing for Kitchens and Bathrooms

Countertop refinishing is one of our most requested services. Laminate, cultured marble, tile and worn stone counters can be resurfaced in a new color and finish without tearing out the old top. We repair chips and burns, fill seams, etch the surface for adhesion and apply durable coatings that resist heat, stains and daily wear. The result is a clean, modern countertop at a fraction of replacement cost.

How Reglazing Works

Reglazing, also called refinishing or resurfacing, restores the glossy finish of a worn fixture. The technician cleans away soap scum and oils, repairs chips and cracks, and etches the surface so the new coating bonds firmly. A primer is applied, followed by professional grade topcoats in your chosen color. The finish cures into a hard, smooth surface that is easy to clean. Tub chip repair and bathtub crack repair are part of the preparation when needed.

The same process works on porcelain, fiberglass, cultured marble and ceramic tile. Bathroom tile reglazing can change dated pink, green or yellow tile to a crisp white or modern neutral. Shower reglazing seals worn surfaces and gives them a uniform look. Sink refinishing and vanity resurfacing complete the bathroom, while countertop refinishing updates the kitchen.

Caring for Your New Finish

A reglazed surface lasts longest with gentle care. Use non abrasive cleaners and soft sponges, avoid harsh chemicals, fix dripping faucets and wipe the surface after use. Do not use suction mats or rubber bath mats that trap moisture against the finish. With simple habits, your refinished tub, tile or countertop can stay beautiful for many years.
